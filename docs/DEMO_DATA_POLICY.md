# SAATHI-AI Demo Data & Privacy Policy

---

## 1. Strictly Synthetic and Anonymized Data
- **Zero Real Personal Data (PII)**: All names, phone numbers, addresses, Aadhaar numbers, identifiers, voice samples, and case records used across SAATHI-AI are 100% synthetically generated or strictly anonymized.
- **Realistic Indian Demographics**: Sample cases, caller personas, and district references (e.g., Gorakhpur, Varanasi, Pune, Jaipur) use realistic Indian nomenclature to ensure operational fidelity during demonstrations, but represent zero real individuals or incidents.

---

## 2. No Production Access Claims
- **Hackathon Demonstration Scope**: This project is developed as a hackathon prototype for Smart India Hackathon (SIH26093).
- **No NHAA / Agency Data Access**: There is no access to, nor claim of access to, live or private production datasets, internal helpline databases, or records from the National Highway Authority / Helpline Authorities (NHAA) or any state/central governmental agency.

---

## 3. Data Protection Safeguards
- **In-Memory & Local Mock Datasets**: In development and demonstration environments, datasets reside locally in SQLite / simulated vectors.
- **Redaction by Design**: Future AI ingestion pipelines are designed to enforce automated Personally Identifiable Information (PII) masking (entity redaction for names, locations, contact numbers) prior to vector indexing or inference.
