# SAATHI-AI Safety & Ethical Principles

SAATHI-AI is engineered with strict ethical boundaries and safety guardrails to ensure technology empowers human operators without compromising the safety, dignity, and agency of callers.

---

## 1. Absolute Prohibition of Clinical Diagnoses
- **No Diagnostic Claims**: The system **never** diagnoses PTSD, depression, anxiety, trauma, psychiatric conditions, or any mental illness.
- **Support, Not Medicine**: SAATHI-AI is a triage and workflow optimization assistant, not a clinical diagnostic instrument or psychiatric evaluation tool.

---

## 2. Nature of the Stress Vulnerability Index (SVI)
- **Triage-Priority Signal Only**: SVI (0–100) is strictly an operational triage signal designed to highlight immediate conversational intensity, urgency, and resource allocation needs.
- **Not Medical or Legal Evidence**: SVI is neither a clinical assessment nor a legal determination of victimhood, credibility, or state of mind.

---

## 3. Neutrality & No Legal/Factual Adjudication
- **No Guilt/Innocence Determination**: The platform never determines guilt, innocence, liability, or veracity of statements made by callers or third parties.
- **Unbiased Reporting**: The AI summarizes factual utterances and emotional tone without introducing subjective moral or legal evaluations.

---

## 4. Transparent & Explainable AI Recommendations
- **Mandatory Justifications**: Every AI output (classification, recommendation, risk flag) must provide:
  - **Contributing Factors**: Clear bullet points explaining why the recommendation was generated.
  - **Supporting Evidence**: Direct references or citations to caller statements or timestamps.
  - **Confidence Score**: Quantitative confidence metric (e.g., 85% confidence based on linguistic matches).

---

## 5. Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop & Logged Overrides
- **Final Decision Authority**: A trained human operator or supervisor retains 100% authority for all decisions, including dispatch, escalation, closing, or re-categorization.
- **Audit Logging of Overrides**: Operators can override any AI suggestion at any time. All overrides, along with optional operator notes, are permanently recorded in the immutable audit log for compliance and continuous system refinement.
